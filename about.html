About Skyler

More than buying and selling.

For me, real estate is about helping people navigate important life transitions with confidence. I combine a decade of enterprise sales experience in the technology industry with deep Vermont roots, a thoughtful data-driven approach, and the personal attention every client deserves.

Before entering real estate, I spent more than ten years in San Francisco leading complex enterprise sales initiatives for technology companies. That experience sharpened my skills in negotiation, strategic planning, and client advocacy — skills I now apply to helping buyers and sellers throughout Northwest Vermont.

Vermont roots, local expertise

Vermont roots, lived daily.

Raised in Jericho, Vermont, I developed an early appreciation for the communities, landscapes, and lifestyle that make the Champlain Valley such a desirable place to live. I attended the University of Vermont, where I was a two-time captain of the men’s soccer team. The leadership, discipline, and teamwork I developed during my collegiate career continue to shape my professional approach today.

After building a successful career in California, my wife and I felt the pull of home and returned to Vermont to raise our family. Today, we live in South Burlington with our two young sons and are proud to be active members of the local community. As a homeowner, parent, and longtime resident, I bring firsthand knowledge of Chittenden County’s neighborhoods, schools, recreation opportunities, and community dynamics — valuable insight for both local and relocating clients.

Business acumen meets modern real estate

Market intelligence and advocacy.

I earned my MBA from the University of Denver, which gave me a strong foundation in market analysis, finance, and strategic decision-making. Combined with my background in technology sales, I offer clients a modern real estate experience rooted in market intelligence, innovative marketing, and skilled negotiation.

My background as an alumnus of Stratton Mountain School and former member of the U.S. Ski Team also gives me unique insight into Vermont’s mountain and resort communities, including Stowe and the surrounding region.
