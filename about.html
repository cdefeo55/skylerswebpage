My approach

Beyond the transaction.

For me, real estate is about more than buying and selling homes. It is about helping people navigate important life transitions with confidence, structure, and clear information.

Before real estate, I spent more than ten years in San Francisco leading complex enterprise sales initiatives for technology companies. That work required preparation, negotiation discipline, stakeholder management, market awareness, and the ability to guide high-value decisions without losing sight of the client’s goals.

I also earned my MBA from the University of Denver, which strengthened the analytical side of my work: pricing, financial context, market evaluation, strategic decision-making, and negotiation planning.

Today, I bring that business foundation back home to Vermont. Whether a client is comparing Burlington-area neighborhoods, evaluating a Lake Champlain property, considering a mountain home in Stowe or Stratton, relocating to the state, or selling a long-held family property, my goal is to make the process more informed, more strategic, and less overwhelming.

Vermont-wide guidance

From lake communities to mountain markets.

Vermont is not one real estate market. A Burlington condo, a Shelburne estate, a Charlotte property with land, a Stowe mountain home, and a Stratton resort property each require a different lens. I help clients understand the market they are actually in, the buyer pool they are trying to reach, and the lifestyle value that supports the property.

Residential communities

Guidance for primary homes, relocation, established neighborhoods, village properties, and homes where daily life, schools, commute, and community rhythm matter.

Lake and land

Mountain and resort

Advice for second-home and resort buyers weighing seasonality, rental rules, HOA documents, access, storage, amenities, and ease of ownership.

Seller advisory

Preparation, pricing, and positioning matter.

When I advise a seller, I separate the market factors we cannot control from the details we can. Location, recent comparable sales, economic conditions, inventory, and absorption rate shape the market backdrop. Pricing, presentation, access, condition, timing, terms, and the property’s story are where preparation can make a meaningful difference.

Pricing discipline

A strong pricing strategy is designed to create confidence and urgency without sacrificing value. The goal is to enter the market in a position that attracts the right buyer pool during the most important early window of activity.

Launch preparation

Before a home goes live, the basics matter: curb appeal, clean and open interiors, light, organization, minor repairs, accessible showings, and clear presentation of the property’s strongest attributes.

Negotiation readiness

My background in enterprise sales and negotiation helps me prepare for the moving parts of a sale: buyer motivation, terms, contingencies, inspection strategy, financing strength, communication, and timing.
