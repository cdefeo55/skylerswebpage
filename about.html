The Story

From the start gate to the closing table.

Raised in Jericho, I developed an early appreciation for the communities, landscapes, and lifestyle of the Champlain Valley. I attended Stratton Mountain School and went on to compete as a former member of the U.S. Ski Team — racing nationally and internationally, where I learned to prepare before the pressure arrives and stay calm when a hundredth of a second decides the outcome.

At the University of Vermont, I competed as a Division I soccer player and two-time team captain, deepening my roots in the state. I then spent more than a decade in San Francisco leading complex enterprise technology sales — work built on preparation, negotiation discipline, and guiding high-value decisions.

Along the way, I earned an MBA from the University of Denver, strengthening the analytical side of my work: pricing, market evaluation, financial context, and strategic decision-making.

Today I bring that foundation home to Vermont. I live in South Burlington with my wife and two young sons, advising buyers and sellers with Four Seasons Sotheby's International Realty — pairing business discipline with genuine local knowledge so every client moves with confidence and clarity.
