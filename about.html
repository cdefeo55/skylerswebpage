About Skyler

More than buying and selling.

For Skyler Davis, real estate is about helping people navigate important life transitions with confidence. Combining a decade of enterprise sales experience in the technology industry with deep Vermont roots, Skyler delivers a thoughtful, data-driven approach while providing the personal attention every client deserves.

Before entering real estate, Skyler spent more than ten years in San Francisco leading complex enterprise sales initiatives for technology companies. That experience sharpened his skills in negotiation, strategic planning, and client advocacy — skills he now applies to helping buyers and sellers achieve exceptional results throughout Northwest Vermont.

Vermont roots, local expertise

Vermont roots, lived daily.

Raised in Jericho, Vermont, Skyler developed an early appreciation for the communities, landscapes, and lifestyle that make the Champlain Valley such a desirable place to live. He attended the University of Vermont, where he was a two-time captain of the men's soccer team. The leadership, discipline, and teamwork he developed during his collegiate career continue to shape his professional approach today.

After building a successful career in California, Skyler and his wife felt the pull of home and returned to Vermont to raise their family. Today, they live in South Burlington with their two young sons and are proud to be active members of the local community. As a homeowner, parent, and longtime resident, Skyler brings firsthand knowledge of Chittenden County's neighborhoods, schools, recreation opportunities, and community dynamics — valuable insight for both local and relocating clients.

Business acumen meets modern real estate

Market intelligence and advocacy.

Skyler earned his MBA from the University of Denver, providing a strong foundation in market analysis, finance, and strategic decision-making. Combined with his background in technology sales, he offers clients a modern real estate experience rooted in market intelligence, innovative marketing, and skilled negotiation.

His background as an alumnus of Stratton Mountain School and former member of the U.S. Ski Team also provides unique insight into Vermont's mountain and resort communities, including Stowe and the surrounding region.
