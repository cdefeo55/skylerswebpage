Pricing approach

My pricing work starts with relevant comparables, then adjusts for condition, setting, scarcity, buyer pool, presentation, and the story the property can credibly tell in the market.

Seller preparation

How to prepare a South Burlington Homes home for market.

Before listing, I help sellers focus on the details most likely to influence buyer perception: curb appeal, light, cleanliness, repairs, storage, showing access, and the way the home’s most compelling features are presented. In a Vermont market, buyers are often evaluating lifestyle as much as square footage, so the home needs to feel easy to understand, easy to tour, and easy to imagine living in.

Control the controllable

We cannot control the broader economy, competing inventory, or every recent sale, but we can control launch preparation, pricing discipline, presentation quality, availability for showings, and how clearly the property’s advantages are communicated.

Make the first impression count

Fresh entryways, clean surfaces, organized closets, tidy outdoor spaces, repaired small items, and simple staging decisions can help buyers focus on the home rather than distractions.

Price with the market, not emotion

The strongest pricing strategy considers relevant comparables, current competition, condition, buyer demand, timing, and how quickly attention can shift after the first weeks on market.
