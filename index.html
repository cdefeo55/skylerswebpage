My Background

Built to perform.
Grounded in Vermont.

Raised in Jericho, I grew up with an early appreciation for the Champlain Valley. I went on to compete as a Division I soccer player and two-time captain at the University of Vermont, and as a member of the US Ski Team — experiences that taught me to prepare before the pressure arrives and stay calm when decisions matter most.

I then spent more than a decade in San Francisco leading complex enterprise technology sales, and earned an MBA from the University of Denver — sharpening the analytical side of my work: pricing, market evaluation, negotiation, and strategy. Today I bring that foundation home to Vermont, where I live in South Burlington with my wife and two young sons, advising clients with Four Seasons Sotheby's International Realty.
